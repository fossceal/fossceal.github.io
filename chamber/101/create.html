What is Create 101?

Create 101 is a chamber built for beginners who want to explore design and creation without pressure. We believe learning happens best by doing—so our sessions focus on hands-on challenges, design logic, and real-world tools like Figma. From understanding visual fundamentals to thinking like a designer, Create 101 helps members grow from curious beginners into confident creators.
